How it works: after starting, the system calibrates to your natural resting head position for 2 seconds. Looking down beyond 12 continuous seconds, or left/right/up at all, counts toward the Rule 1 gaze ledger — 2 warnings, then termination on the 3rd. Every 4 seconds, keyboard/click activity is cross-checked against how still your body is; sustained input with no matching motion is flagged as a possible ghost-writer, 2 warnings then a locked termination on the 3rd. A lightweight vision model watches for phones in frame — any hit is a 3-second blackout and instant termination, zero tolerance. Multiple faces allow 3 warnings before ending on the 4th; tab switches (including minimizing the window) allow 3 warnings before ending the session on the 4th.

[ PERMITTED ]

  • Keep your face centered and visible in the frame at all times.
  • Glance down briefly for scratchpad work, then return your eyes to the screen.
  • Keep both hands visible near the keyboard.
  • Sit in a well-lit room, facing the camera directly.
  • Keep this tab active and in focus for the whole session.
  • Type at your own natural pace.

[ FLAGGED ]

  • Don't look down for more than 12 seconds at a stretch.
  • Don't glance left, right, or upward toward notes or a second screen.
  • Don't keep any phone, calculator, or tablet in the camera's view.
  • Don't switch tabs, minimize the window, or open other apps.
  • Don't let anyone else type or click on your behalf.
  • Don't allow a second face to appear in frame, even briefly.
